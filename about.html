Our Story

Founded in 2026, EventHub was born from a simple observation: great events were happening everywhere, but discovering them was a challenge. We set out to change that.

What started as a small team of 5 passionate event enthusiasts in Bhubaneswar has now grown into a thriving platform serving thousands of users across India. We've partnered with over 500 event organizers and hosted 500+ successful events.

Today, EventHub stands as a testament to our belief that every moment deserves to be celebrated, and every experience should be unforgettable.
