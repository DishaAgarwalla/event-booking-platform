Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book tickets?
Simply browse events, click on "View Details", then "Book Now", fill in your details, and complete the checkout process.
Can I cancel my booking?
Yes, cancellations are allowed up to 48 hours before the event. A small cancellation fee may apply.
How do I get my ticket?
After successful booking, you can download your ticket from the confirmation page. You'll also receive an email with your ticket.
Are there refunds for no-shows?
No refunds are provided for no-shows. Please cancel before the deadline if you cannot attend.
